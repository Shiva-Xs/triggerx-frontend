Ethereum price alerts

Pick an ETH level, pick a direction, and TriggerX messages you the moment the market crosses it, by email, Telegram, or both.

TriggerX watches ETHUSDT on the live Binance Spot feed. You can arm several ETH levels at once, which is the usual reason people want alerts on Ethereum rather than just Bitcoin: there is often more than one number worth knowing about.

Set an ETH alert →

Multiple levels, one pair

Each alert is independent, and you can hold up to 50 active alerts per account. In practice that means you can bracket a range, with one below alert underneath and one above alert on top, and hear about whichever side resolves first, without the two interfering.

If you would rather cover both sides of a single level with one alert, use crosses, which fires from either direction.

Percentage moves

ETH up 8% is a valid alert. The live price is read when you arm it, the target is computed from that, and the alert sits at the resulting number. This saves the arithmetic when what you actually care about is the size of the move rather than a specific figure.

Because the percentage resolves at creation time, an alert armed at a different moment produces a different target. It is a convenience for setting a level, not a trailing stop.

Where alerts arrive

  • Email. The subject line alone tells you what happened, so you do not have to open it.
  • Telegram. Link once to @TriggerX_AlertBot and alerts land in that chat, where you can also add and list alerts by command.
  • Chrome extension. Reads the pair from an open Binance or TradingView tab so you can arm an ETH alert without retyping the symbol.

Coverage beyond ETH

Every active USDT pair on Binance is supported, several hundred of them, and the list refreshes on a schedule so newly listed coins become available without any action from you. The same conditions and plain-English input work identically on any of them.

TriggerX is built to inform, not to replace your trading safeguards. No alert service can guarantee delivery every time. Network interruptions, exchange feed delays and downtime can all make a notification late or missed. For risk that actually matters, use exchange-native stop-losses or limit orders, which execute without depending on you reading a message.

Questions

How do I set an Ethereum price alert?

Sign in with an emailed one-time code, choose ETHUSDT, and give a price plus a direction: above, below, or crosses. Plain English also works, for example ETH below 2500.

Can I alert on an ETH percentage move?

Yes. Type something like ETH up 8% and the live price at creation time is used to compute the target. The percentage is resolved once when the alert is armed rather than trailing the price.

Can I watch ETH and other pairs at the same time?

Yes, up to 50 active alerts per account across any supported pairs. Each alert is independent, so ETH can have several levels armed at once.

Which Ethereum pair is used for the price?

ETHUSDT on Binance Spot. Every active USDT pair on Binance is supported, and the tradable list refreshes automatically so new listings appear without any action from you.
