Crypto price alerts in Telegram

Link once to @TriggerX_AlertBot and your alerts arrive as Telegram messages, with the whole alert list manageable from that same chat, by command or plain English.

Telegram is the fastest of the delivery channels and the only one where you can also create alerts, not just receive them. For anyone who already keeps Telegram open, it removes the dashboard from the loop entirely.

Link Telegram →

Commands

  • /add creates an alert from natural language, e.g. /add BTC above 90000.
  • /list shows every alert you currently have armed.
  • /history lists the last ten alerts that fired.
  • /price BTC checks a live price without arming anything.
  • /deleteall clears all active alerts at once.

Why the chat is a good place for this

An alert is a single short fact (a pair, a level, a direction) and a chat thread is a natural format for it. Everything you have armed and everything that has fired sits in one scrollable history, so /list answers "what am I waiting on" without opening anything else.

It also means alerts land in the same place you already get notifications, with your existing mute and priority settings applying to them.

Linking your account

  1. Sign in on the web. A one-time emailed code, no password.
  2. Open the Telegram link step from the dashboard and follow it to the bot.
  3. Start the chat. The link is confirmed and the channel is live.
  4. Use the chat from then on. Add, list and delete alerts without returning to the site.

Telegram alongside email

The channels are independent, so you can have both. Both are triggered at the same instant server-side; Telegram simply tends to arrive first, because email passes through more infrastructure on the way. Using both is the closest thing to redundancy the service offers.

TriggerX is built to inform, not to replace your trading safeguards. No alert service can guarantee delivery every time. Network interruptions, exchange feed delays and downtime can all make a notification late or missed, and that applies to Telegram as much as to email. For risk that actually matters, use exchange-native stop-losses or limit orders.

Questions

How do I get crypto price alerts in Telegram?

Link your account to @TriggerX_AlertBot once from the dashboard. After that, alerts are delivered as Telegram messages and you can create and manage them from the chat itself.

What commands does the Telegram bot support?

/add creates an alert from natural language, /list shows active alerts, /history returns the last ten that fired, /price BTC checks a live price, and /deleteall clears every alert.

Is Telegram faster than email for alerts?

In practice yes. Telegram delivery is close to instant, while email usually lands within a few seconds because it passes through additional mail infrastructure. Both are triggered at the same moment server-side.

Can I use Telegram and email at the same time?

Yes. The channels are not exclusive, so an alert can notify you in both places if you want redundancy.
