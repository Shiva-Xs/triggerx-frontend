Set a price alert on any crypto available on Binance Spot and get notified the instant the market hits your target. TriggerX sends instant email and Telegram alerts for Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL) and 400+ trading pairs - completely free, open source, no password needed. 400+ pairs, under 2 second latency, $0 always.

Five things, done perfectly

Live price monitoring

Prices stream live from Binance over WebSocket. Every active USDT pair, from BTC to hundreds of altcoins, updates in under one second, continuously, 24/7. TriggerX watches them all so you never have to.

Three alert conditions

Alert when a price goes above your target, drops below it, or crosses it from either direction. The crosses condition watches both sides so you don't need two separate alerts.

Set alerts in plain English

No forms. Just type it: "ETH below 2500" or "BTC up 10%". TriggerX parses the intent and arms the alert automatically.

Percentage-based alerts

Don't know the exact price? Just say the move. "Alert me when BTC is up 10%." TriggerX reads the live price, works out the target, and sets the alert for you. No math required.

One alert, one notification

When your condition is met, you get notified once. No repeated pings, no duplicates. The alert deactivates cleanly after it fires and moves to your history.

Common questions

How fast are alerts?
Prices stream live from Binance with no polling delay. When a price crosses your target the notification goes out within the same second. Telegram is nearly instant. Email typically arrives within a few seconds.
Can I rely on TriggerX for critical trades?
TriggerX is designed to be fast and reliable, but no alert service can guarantee delivery 100% of the time. Network interruptions, exchange feed delays, or unexpected downtime can occasionally cause a notification to fire late or be missed. Always use exchange-native stop-losses or limit orders for risk that matters. TriggerX is built to inform, not to replace your trading safeguards.
What coins are supported?
Every active USDT trading pair on Binance, hundreds of coins. The list updates automatically every few hours so new listings are picked up without any action from you.
Is it free?
Completely free and open source. No credit card, no trial, no hidden tier. The code is on GitHub and will stay that way.
Do I need to keep a tab open?
No. TriggerX runs on our servers, not your browser. Set an alert, close everything. You'll get notified the moment the condition is met.
How many alerts can I set at once?
50 active alerts per account. That covers every serious trading setup. If you need more, reach out.
